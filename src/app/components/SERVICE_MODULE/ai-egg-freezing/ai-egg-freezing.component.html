What is AI Egg-Freezing?

Preserve Your Fertility with Advanced AI Technology

AI Egg-Freezing is an advanced fertility technique where a woman's eggs (oocytes) are retrieved, analyzed, and frozen using Artificial Intelligence (AI) assisted technologies. AI helps in selecting the healthiest eggs for freezing, improving future IVF success rates and preserving fertility for women who want to delay pregnancy.

How it works

Ovarian stimulation

Hormonal treatments stimulate the ovaries to produce multiple eggs.

Egg retrieval

Eggs are collected from the ovaries using a minimally invasive procedure.

AI-assisted egg analysis

AI evaluates egg quality and selects the best eggs for freezing.

Cryopreservation

Selected eggs are frozen using advanced vitrification methods for long-term storage.

Benefits

Preserves fertility for women who wish to delay pregnancy.

AI improves egg selection for higher chances of future IVF success.

Minimally invasive procedure with high safety standards.

Provides flexibility and reproductive autonomy for women.
