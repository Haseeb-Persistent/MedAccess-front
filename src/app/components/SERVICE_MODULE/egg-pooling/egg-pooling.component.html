What is Egg-Pooling in AI-IVF?

Maximize Success with Smart Embryo Storage

AI-Assisted Egg pooling is an AI-IVF strategy where multiple oocyte (egg) collection cycles are performed, with the resulting embryos being frozen and stored together for a single future transfer. This approach is often recommended for patients with low ovarian reserves or those undergoing preimplantation genetic testing, as it increases the number of available embryos to choose from. It can lead to more treatment with less cost and higher success rates per embryo transfer in AI-IVF.

How it works

Multiple cycles

The patient undergoes ovarian stimulation and egg retrieval over two or more AI-IVF cycles.

Embryo creation and freezing

After each retrieval, eggs are fertilized, and the resulting embryos are frozen and cryopreserved.

Pooling and transfer

Once a sufficient number of embryos have been collected, they are thawed together, and the best ones are selected for transfer into the uterus.

Monitoring

The uterine lining is monitored for the correct thickness before the transfer can take place.

Benefits of Egg Pooling

Increased chances of success

By collecting more embryos, there is a larger pool to select from, improving the probability of achieving a successful positive pregnancy.

More efficient for low ovarian reserve

Maximizes the chances of obtaining viable embryos over time for patients with diminished egg supply.
