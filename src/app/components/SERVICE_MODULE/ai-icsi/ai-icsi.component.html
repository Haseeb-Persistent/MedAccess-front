What is AI-ICSI?

Next-Generation Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection

AI-ICSI is a method in (ART) Assisted Reproductive Technology where Artificial Intelligence (AI) is used to automate and improve the process of Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI), a type of advanced IVF. Instead of a human embryologist, an AI-guided system selects the best sperm, immobilizes it, and injects it into an egg — leading to potential benefits like increased speed, precision, and the possibility of remote operations.

How it works

Automation of ICSI

The AI system takes over the typically manual ICSI procedure, which involves many steps including sperm immobilization with a laser and direct injection into the egg’s cytoplasm.

AI-powered sperm selection

An AI system analyzes images to identify and select high-quality sperm for fertilization, often with more speed and precision than a human can achieve.
