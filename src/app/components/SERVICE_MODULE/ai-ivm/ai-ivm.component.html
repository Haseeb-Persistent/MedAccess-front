What is AI-IVM?

Advanced In Vitro Maturation Technology

IVM stands for In Vitro Maturation, which is a process of collecting immature eggs from the ovaries and maturing them in our AI-IVF lab before they are fertilized. It is a less invasive alternative to traditional IVF because it requires significantly fewer hormone injections.

How it works?

Low-Dose Stimulation

Immature eggs are collected by an ART surgeon from the ovaries after a brief, low-dose hormonal stimulation.

AI-Powered Lab Maturation

In the AI-powered IVF lab, the eggs are matured in a growth medium, sometimes with added hormones, for one to two days.

Fertilization

Once mature, the eggs are fertilized using techniques like ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection).

Note: It's a powerful solution for LOW AMH & older women. Even at 55, motherhood is possible in our AI-IVF lab.
