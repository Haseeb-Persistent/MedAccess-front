Stem Cell Ovarian Rejuvenation?

Restoring Ovarian Function Through Advanced Science

How it works

Stem cell preparation

A sample of a patient's blood or adipose tissue is processed to isolate stem cells.

Injection

The concentrated mix is injected directly into the ovaries, typically under ultrasound guidance.

Mechanism of action

The growth factors and signaling molecules from the stem cells trigger a local healing response that can activate dormant follicles, promote tissue repair, improve ovarian function, make the face look younger, activate daily life energy, and restore hormonal balance.

Potential benefits

Improved ovarian reserve

May lead to an increase in follicle growth and hormone levels like AMH.

Enhanced IVF success

Can potentially improve the response to IVF stimulation and increase the chances of conception.

Hormonal balance

May help restore ovarian function and improve hormonal balance.

Restored menstrual cycles

Some women have experienced a return of menstrual cycles after treatment.

Who may be a candidate

Women with poor ovarian reserve or low AMH levels.

Women with premature ovarian failure or insufficiency.

Women who have had previous unsuccessful IVF attempts.
