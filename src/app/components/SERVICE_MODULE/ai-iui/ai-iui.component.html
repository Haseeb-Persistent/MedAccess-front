What is AI-IUI?

Advanced Artificial Intelligence Insemination

AI-IUI combines Artificial Intelligence (AI) with Intrauterine Insemination (IUI). IUI is an advanced fertility treatment where prepared sperm is placed directly into the uterus to help achieve pregnancy. AI is being explored to improve IUI outcomes by automatically selecting the healthiest sperm using hi-tech solutions run by ART surgeons.

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

What it is:

A fertility procedure where healthy, washed sperm is placed directly into the uterus using a thin tube or catheter.

Purpose:

To increase the chances of fertilization by bringing sperm closer to the egg during ovulation.

When it's used:

For various forms of infertility, including unexplained infertility, low sperm count or quality, or for same-sex couples.

Procedure:

The doctor places the sperm into the uterus around the time of ovulation.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and IUI

Current role:

AI is a standard part of advanced AI-IUI procedures. It is being researched to improve timing, sperm selection, and other factors to enhance success.

Potential applications:

AI could predict the best timing for IUI, improve sperm selection, and analyze other factors to increase chances of pregnancy.
