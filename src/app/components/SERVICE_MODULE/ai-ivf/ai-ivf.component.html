What is AI-IVF?

The Future of Fertility Treatment

AI-IVF is the advanced integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) processes to improve efficiency and outcomes. AI analyzes vast amounts of data to assist in key steps like embryo selection, genetic screening, and sperm selection — enhancing accuracy, success rate, and reducing human error.

How AI Enhances IVF

Embryo Selection

AI software analyzes embryo images to grade them for higher implantation rates.

Genetic Screening

AI detects genetic abnormalities non-invasively, offering an alternative to PGT.

Semen & Egg Selection

AI identifies the best semen and egg combinations for fertilization.

Personalized Treatment Plans

AI predicts success rates and designs customized protocols.

Automation

AI performs precise lab tasks, reducing human error and increasing consistency.

AI-IVF represents the future of infertility treatment — beyond traditional IVF.
