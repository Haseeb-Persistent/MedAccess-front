What is Gender Selection in
AI-IVF?

Precision and Accuracy in Family Planning

Gender Selection is an advanced AI-assisted IVF technique that allows parents to select the desired gender of their child before implantation. AI technology is used to analyze embryos and ensure accurate selection based on genetic information.

How it works

Embryo creation

Eggs and sperm are fertilized in the lab using IVF procedures.

Genetic analysis

AI analyzes the embryos to determine their genetic makeup, including gender chromosomes.

Selection

The best embryos of the desired gender are selected for implantation.

Implantation

The selected embryo is transferred to the uterus to achieve pregnancy.

Benefits

Supports families in planning pregnancies with specific gender-related considerations.

Increases the chance of having a child of the desired gender.

Uses AI for higher accuracy and success rates, rather than old IVF methods.
